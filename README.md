## This is not a productivity app.

Productivity apps want your tasks. LifeOS wants to know who you're becoming.

There is no inbox to zero. No streak to be shamed by. No red badge shouting at you from a home screen. LifeOS was built on a simple belief: **a life, observed with care, tends to improve on its own.** The job of the software is not to push — it's to make the pattern visible, and then get out of the way.

Open it and you should feel something closer to opening a leather journal inside a quiet observatory than logging into a dashboard. If it ever starts to feel like software, we've failed.

<br>

## ◈ What It Believes

| | |
|---|---|
| **One source of truth** | Every fact about your life exists exactly once. Everything else is a reflection of it. |
| **Local-first** | Your life is not a subscription. It lives on your device, offline, always. Cloud sync is a convenience, never a dependency. |
| **Private by default** | No ads. No trackers. No "engagement." You are not the product being optimized — your life is. |
| **Calm by design** | If a screen can't reduce anxiety, it doesn't ship. Whitespace is a feature. Silence is a design element. |
| **Emotional, not transactional** | You're not managing tasks. You're watching yourself grow, one small, quiet page at a time. |

<br>

## ◈ The Rituals

LifeOS doesn't have "sessions." It has rituals — small returns, at the edges of the day.

- **Morning** — the app greets you, and shows you today, gently
- **Evening** — a quiet reflection, not an interrogation
- **Weekly** — a reset, a breath
- **Monthly** — a review, in full
- **Yearly** — evolution, made visible
